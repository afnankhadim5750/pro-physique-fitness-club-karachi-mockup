Programmes

Five ways to train on this floor. Pick your goal, show up, put the work in. Class timings aren’t published — call the gym to ask what’s on this week.

P—01

Strength

Barbell work, bench, squats and deadlifts. Heavy days, built on the basics, with a coach watching your form.

P—02

Conditioning

Ropes, sled-style effort and high-intensity rounds that build engine and grit — short, sharp, honest work.

P—03

Cardio

Treadmills and bikes for warm-ups, fat-loss sessions and steady-state days. Start slow, build the habit.

P—04

Boxing & bag work

Heavy-bag rounds for fitness, coordination and stress relief. Gloves on, phone off, three minutes at a time.

P—05

Mobility & core

Stretching, mat work and core sessions that keep joints moving and backs healthy between heavy days.
